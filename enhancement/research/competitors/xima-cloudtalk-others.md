# Competitor Deep Dive — Modern Wallboard / CCaaS Platforms

These are the lighter-weight CCaaS/analytics tools we'll compete with on **wallboards, contact-center metrics, and AI** while differentiating with **multi-PBX + billing + MENA-native**.

---

## Xima
- **Position:** Top call center reporting & analytics suite for 2026
- **Strengths:** depth in Cisco UCCX-style analytics; mature reports
- **Weaknesses:** US-focused; no billing; limited multi-PBX
- **What to copy:** report library breadth
- **Beat with:** multi-PBX + billing + AI + MENA

## CloudTalk
- **Position:** call center software with CRM-focused integrations
- **Pricing:** $19-99/user/mo; AI Voice Agents at $350/month for 1,000 minutes
- **Strengths:** clean UI, strong CRM ecosystem, simple pricing
- **Weaknesses:** is its own PBX (not PBX-agnostic), limited billing
- **What to copy:** clean SaaS pricing; AI minutes pricing model
- **Beat with:** PBX-agnostic ("don't change phone provider"), MENA pricing

## Dialpad
- **Position:** AI-powered CCaaS with built-in PBX
- **Strengths:** strong AI (transcription, sentiment, summaries), wallboards
- **Weaknesses:** locked to Dialpad PBX, no MENA presence
- **Beat with:** PBX-agnostic, multi-currency

## Analytics 365 / Tollring — see `tollring.md`

## ISI Analytics
- **Position:** Microsoft Teams Call Queue + Auto Attendant analytics
- **Strengths:** deep Teams reporting (call volume, abandonment, AHT, ASA, full call routing paths)
- **Weaknesses:** Teams-only
- **Beat with:** add 3CX, Cisco, Webex, Zoom

## Teams PowerPack
- **Position:** suite of three native Teams reporting solutions
- **Strengths:** wallboards, automated reports, 90-day retention; native Teams feel
- **Weaknesses:** Teams-only; limited retention; no billing or recording
- **Beat with:** longer retention, multi-PBX, recording, billing

## Momentum
- **Position:** Microsoft Teams call analytics
- **Strengths:** detailed metrics
- **Weaknesses:** Teams-only

## KrispCall
- **Position:** startup-friendly contact center
- **Strengths:** simple, low-cost
- **Weaknesses:** limited features, own PBX

## Nextiva, RingCentral, Vonage, 8x8
- **Position:** full UCaaS suites with analytics built in
- **Note:** these are NOT direct competitors (they sell PBX too)
- **Position vs. them:** "we sit on top of any PBX, you don't have to switch carriers"

---

## CallCenterDashboards.com
- **Position:** 3CX-specific wallboards + reports
- **Direct competitor on 3CX dashboards**
- **Beat with:** multi-PBX (their wedge is 3CX-only), AI, billing, MENA

## Calltools / Convoso / Five9 / Talkdesk / Genesys / NICE inContact
- Full CCaaS suites; competitors at enterprise level
- **Beat with:** multi-PBX-agnostic, MENA, lower TCO
