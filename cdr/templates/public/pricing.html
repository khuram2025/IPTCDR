FAQ

Common questions

Does it work with my 3CX?

Yes — Zentryc connects to 3CX V20 via its call feed and read-only API. Cisco CUCM and cloud platforms are on the roadmap with the same dashboards.

Is my data isolated?

Every tenant is strictly isolated, with role-based access, audit logging and per-tenant IP allow-listing.

How fast can we go live?

Most call centers are live within days — connect the PBX, we normalize the data, and your dashboards populate.

Do you support Arabic?

Yes — full right-to-left layout and localization, built for KSA and the GCC.
