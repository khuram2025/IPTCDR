# Vertical — Healthcare Contact Centers

## Why this vertical
- Hospital chains, clinic networks, telemedicine platforms in MENA growing fast
- Patient access (after-hours, telemedicine) is a CX battleground
- Compliance pressure: patient privacy + recording rules
- KSA Vision 2030 emphasis on healthcare modernization
- Avg deal size: SAR 30,000-300,000/year for hospital contact center

## Use cases

### Patient access (inbound)
- Appointment booking
- Lab results inquiry
- Prescription refills
- Emergency triage
- Insurance / billing questions
- Telehealth handoff

### Patient outreach (outbound)
- Appointment confirmations / reminders
- Medication adherence calls
- Post-discharge follow-up
- Survey / satisfaction
- Preventive care campaigns

## Key features for healthcare buyers (2026)

### Routing & access
- **Priority-based routing** — urgent (lab abnormal, ER) before routine
- **Skill-based routing** — by specialty (cardiology, pediatrics, women's health)
- **Multi-channel** — voice, SMS, WhatsApp, web chat (patient prefers their channel)
- **Predictive routing** — analyze patient context + history to route to best agent
- **24/7 after-hours** workflows (on-call rotation, triage IVR)

### Telemedicine integration
- One-click handoff from voice IVR to video consult (Zoom / Teams meeting link via SMS)
- Calendar sync with physician schedules
- Pre-consult intake bot collects symptoms

### Privacy & compliance
- **PII / PHI redaction** in recordings (patient name, MRN, diagnosis if needed)
- **Consent capture** at call start
- **Per-call retention** rules (e.g., 7 years for clinical, 3 for admin)
- **Access controls** — only treating clinicians + admin

### AI features
- Auto-summary of patient call → push to EMR
- Sentiment to flag distressed patients
- Topic spotting (e.g., "side effect", "pain", "lawsuit")
- Multi-language: Arabic (MSA + Saudi/Egyptian/Levantine), English, Urdu, Tagalog (common care-staff languages)

### Workforce management
- Forecast appointment volumes (seasonal: flu, allergies, Hajj)
- Multi-skill scheduling (specialty queues)
- Shift swap for nurses

### Reporting
- Avg time to schedule appointment
- After-hours call volume
- Abandoned call rate (critical metric)
- First call resolution
- Patient satisfaction (NPS, CSAT)
- Referral source attribution
