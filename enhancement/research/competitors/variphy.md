# Competitor Deep Dive — Variphy

## Snapshot
- **Founded:** est. 2007 (Cisco-focused from outset)
- **Headquarters:** USA
- **Reach:** 1,500+ orgs, 4M+ phones, 30+ countries
- **Position:** #1 Cisco Collaboration analytics & management platform

## Product modules
- CUCM CDR Reporting & Call Analytics
- UCCX Reporting & Wallboards
- Cisco CUBE CDR Reporting
- Webex Calling Reporting & Analytics
- Webex Meetings reporting
- Microsoft Teams Phone Analytics & Reporting
- Zoom Phone Reporting & Analytics
- Variphy Cloud
- Remote Phone Control / Macros / Broadcast
- Change Management / As-Built Reporting
- DN & DID Inventory Management

## Pricing
- Quote-based; reviewers say "very reasonably priced"
- No public per-user pricing

## Strengths
- Deep Cisco integration (UCCX wallboards, CUBE CDR, dial-plan-aware reporting)
- Customizable dashboards with drag-and-drop widgets
- Strong customer service & support reputation
- Broad platform coverage (Cisco + Webex + Teams + Zoom)
- Mature: 18+ years in market

## Weaknesses
- US-centric distribution and support hours
- Cisco-first DNA (other platforms are newer additions)
- No native Arabic UI
- Enterprise pricing locks out SMB
- Limited AI/conversation-intelligence (sentiment, summary)
- No call recording (must integrate 3rd party)
- No billing/invoicing (reporting only)
- No fraud detection
- No omnichannel

## What to copy
1. **Drag-and-drop widget dashboards** — set the bar for customizability
2. **DID Inventory Management module** — overlooked but valuable enterprise feature
3. **Remote Phone Control + Macros + Broadcast** — phone-fleet management as add-on revenue
4. **Change Management & As-Built Reporting** — audit trail of UC config changes (compliance hook)

## What to beat them on
1. **Multi-PBX from day one (not Cisco-bolt-on)** — clean abstraction
2. **MENA pricing + Arabic UI**
3. **AI conversation intelligence** built in
4. **Billing + recording bundled** (not separate)
5. **Fraud detection** built in
6. **Omnichannel** (they don't do it)

## How to position against them
> "Variphy is the right choice if you're a US Cisco shop with deep CUCM specialization and budget for enterprise quote-based pricing. IPT Portal is the right choice if you need multi-PBX, billing, AI, recording, and fraud detection in one bundle, with native Arabic UI and KSA/UAE payment support, at predictable monthly SaaS pricing."
