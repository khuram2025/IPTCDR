How Call Back Tracking Works

This dashboard tracks when agents call back customers who previously missed IVR calls:

  • Missed Call Detection: Identifies calls that reached IVR but weren't answered by agents
  • Callback Matching: Finds outgoing calls from extensions to numbers that previously missed calls
  • Success Tracking: Monitors whether callbacks were answered and connected
  • Response Time: Measures how quickly agents respond to missed calls
