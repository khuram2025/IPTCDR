About us

We give contact centers the honest numbers

Zentryc was born from a simple frustration: standard PBX reports measure the IVR, not the agent — so they look great while customers wait. We built the platform that tells the truth, in real time, and automates everything around it.

Our mission

Turn call data into decisions

Every queue, every agent, every abandoned call — measured accurately and surfaced the moment it matters, so supervisors can act, finance can bill correctly, and security can stop fraud before it costs you.

  • Real-time by default — wallboards and alerts, not yesterday's CSV
  • Vendor-neutral — one model across 3CX, Cisco and cloud
  • Built for the GCC — Arabic, RTL and local tax
