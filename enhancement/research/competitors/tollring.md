# Competitor Deep Dive — Tollring

## Snapshot
- **Headquarters:** UK (also India)
- **Position:** UC analytics + AI-powered call recording + fraud management + collaboration analytics
- **Reach:** strong UK/EU; partner-channel-led

## Product portfolio
- **Analytics 365** — UC analytics platform (the flagship)
- **iCall Suite** — call analytics for MS Teams + other platforms
- **Tollring Protect** — real-time telecom fraud detection & revenue assurance
- **AI-powered Recording** — voice recording + conversation analysis for Teams calls/meetings
- "Triple Play for Teams" bundling: Analytics + Recording + Fraud

## Pricing
- Subscription-based with three tiers per product:
  - **Essentials** — individual / business insights, single touchpoint
  - **Advanced** — detailed reporting + wallboards + dashboards
  - **Ultimate** — managing customer-facing teams, live wallboards, call centre analytics
- Specific prices not public; partner-customizable
- Bolt-on modules sold separately (recording, contact-center features)

## Strengths
- Strong AI/conversation-intelligence layer
- Real-time + historical insights together
- Fraud product is mature (Tollring Protect)
- Microsoft Teams positioning is strong
- Channel-friendly (partners customize tiers)
- Recent product launches show momentum (iCall Suite for Teams, subscription pivot)

## Weaknesses
- UK/EU-centric
- No native Arabic UI
- Microsoft-Teams-first positioning means weaker on 3CX, CUCM, Zoom, Webex
- No billing/invoicing
- No omnichannel inbox
- Pricing opaque; partner-mediated sales can be slow

## What to copy
1. **Triple Play bundling** — Analytics + Recording + Fraud as one package; we'll do similar with Bill + Insight + Contact
2. **Subscription tier model** (Essentials / Advanced / Ultimate) — clean upgrade path
3. **Real-time + historical in same UI** — don't separate
4. **AI-powered recording** as headline feature
5. **Partner-customizable pricing** — give resellers margin flexibility

## What to beat them on
1. **MENA presence + Arabic**
2. **Multi-PBX support beyond Teams** (we cover 3CX, CUCM, Webex, Zoom, generic SIP)
3. **Convergent billing built in** (they don't bill — they only report)
4. **MENA payment gateways**
5. **More transparent SaaS pricing** for SMB

## How to position against them
> "Tollring is excellent for UK/EU Microsoft Teams customers who need recording + fraud + analytics. IPT Portal is the MENA-native equivalent that also handles 3CX, Cisco, Webex, Zoom, with billing + invoicing built in, Arabic UI, and Mada/HyperPay support."
