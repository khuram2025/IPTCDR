# Business Opportunities

How each new feature converts to a revenue line.

---

## Direct revenue lines (subscriptions)

### 1. **IPT Bill** — Convergent billing & rating SaaS
- **Customers:** SMB, mid-market, MSPs
- **Pricing:** SAR 499 (Starter) → SAR 3,999+ (Pro) per tenant/mo
- **Margin:** 80%+ (pure SaaS)
- **Why win:** real-time rating, multi-PBX, multi-currency, VAT engine, Mada/HyperPay, MENA-first
- **Who buys:** anyone with >25 extensions and any cost-control pain

### 2. **IPT Insight** — UC analytics & reporting
- **Customers:** all PBX-using orgs (3CX, Cisco, Teams, Webex, Zoom)
- **Pricing:** SAR 999 (Standard) → SAR 2,999 (Pro with wallboards & custom reports)
- **Margin:** 85%+
- **Why win:** Variphy-class features at MENA prices, Arabic UI, no Cisco lock-in

### 3. **IPT Contact** — AI contact-center suite
- **Customers:** mid-market & enterprise contact centers, BPOs, banks, hospitals
- **Pricing:** SAR 89-189/agent/mo (typical industry: $25-50/agent)
- **Margin:** 60-75% (after AI/STT costs)
- **Why win:** Arabic+English transcription, SAMA-compliant recording, integrated with billing & wallboards

## Usage-based revenue lines

### 4. **AI minutes** (transcription, sentiment, summarization)
- **Pricing:** SAR 0.10-0.20/min (cost: ~$0.01-0.04/min via Whisper API)
- **Margin:** 60-75%
- **Up-sells:** Arabic premium (~SAR 0.30/min), real-time streaming (~SAR 0.40/min)

### 5. **Recording storage**
- **Pricing:** SAR 0.50/GB/mo cold storage; SAR 2/GB/mo hot
- **Cost:** S3-compatible storage ~$0.005/GB/mo (cold), $0.023/GB/mo (hot)
- **Margin:** 90%+

### 6. **AI voice agent / IVR bot minutes**
- **Pricing:** SAR 1-2/min (industry: $0.20-0.50/min)
- **Margin:** 50-65% after model + STT + TTS costs
- **Up-sells:** Arabic dialect packs, custom voice cloning

### 7. **WhatsApp Business API messaging**
- **Pricing:** SAR 0.10-0.30/conversation (Meta charges ~$0.005-0.07/conv)
- **Margin:** 70-85%

### 8. **SMS messaging** (alerts, OTP, callbacks)
- **Pricing:** SAR 0.15-0.30/msg
- **Margin:** depends on aggregator

### 9. **Outbound dialer minutes** (predictive/power dialer)
- **Pricing:** SAR 0.10/min over carrier cost
- **Margin:** depends on rate-card

## Add-on revenue lines

### 10. **Toll-fraud protection** (P1 quick win)
- **Pricing:** SAR 199-499/mo per tenant (matches WebCDR's $300/mo benchmark)
- **Effort:** ~2 weeks to ship MVP
- **Margin:** 95%+
- **GTM:** "Free Toll-Fraud Audit" lead magnet → convert to subscription

### 11. **Compliance / SAMA-grade recording bundle**
- **Pricing:** SAR 1,500-5,000/mo per tenant (banks, insurers)
- **Includes:** 10-year retention, encryption, audit log, immutable storage, certified backups
- **Margin:** 70-80%

### 12. **Custom report builder / BI integration**
- **Pricing:** SAR 499/mo add-on
- **Includes:** drag-drop builder + Power BI / Tableau / Looker connector

### 13. **CRM connector packs** (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Bitrix24)
- **Pricing:** SAR 199-499/mo per integration
- **Margin:** 90%+
- **Includes:** click-to-call, screen pop, call logging, contact sync

### 14. **API call quotas** (developer / partner tier)
- **Pricing:** SAR 0.001-0.005/call over included tier
- **Use case:** ISVs building on top

### 15. **White-label / reseller platform fee**
- **Pricing:** SAR 5,000-15,000/mo platform fee + 15-30% rev-share

### 16. **Dedicated KSA data residency**

### 17. **Premium support / SLA tiers**
- **Pricing:** 10-20% of base subscription

## Strategic monetization moats

| Moat | Lock-in mechanism |
|---|---|
| Multi-PBX adapters | Customer would need to rebuild integrations elsewhere |
| Stored recordings + transcripts | 10-year retention = 10-year customer |
| Reseller portal | Partners build their own customer base on us |
| MENA payment gateways (Mada, HyperPay) | Hard for foreign vendors to replicate |
| Arabic-tuned AI | Custom STT models tuned on customer audio |
| Compliance certifications (SAMA, CITC, SOC 2) | Procurement gates competitors out |
| Vertical templates | Out-of-box value vs. blank-slate generic platforms |
