# Competitor Deep Dive — Imagicle

## Snapshot
- **Headquarters:** Italy
- **Position:** UCX (Unified Communications eXperience) suite for Cisco + Webex + Microsoft Teams
- **Listed on Cisco GPL S+** — preferential procurement for Cisco customers

## Product modules (UCX Suite)
- **Call Recording** — Always-On / On-Demand / Selective; for Cisco, Teams, Webex
- **Attendant Console** — operator panel with advanced queueing, auto-attendant
- **Call Analytics & Billing** — 50+ reports, cost allocation, data-driven decisions
- **Auto Attendant** — IVR
- **Advanced Queueing**
- **Hybrid environment support**
- **Pre-built and custom integrations** via secure API

## Pricing
- Imagicle Call Recording starts at $1 flat rate per user (entry-level)
- UCX Cloud Suite — single subscription with elastic resources, unlimited data retention
- On Cisco GPL S+ for easier Cisco-customer procurement

## Compliance positioning
- GDPR, MiFID II, HIPAA, PCI-DSS
- Targets regulated industries: finance, healthcare, law

## Strengths
- Suite breadth (recording + console + billing + analytics in one)
- Compliance-grade recording (regulated-industries focus)
- Strong Cisco channel (GPL listing is a big deal)
- Webex App Hub presence
- Italian/EU support culture and language coverage
- Multi-mode recording (Always-On / On-Demand / Selective) — copyable design

## Weaknesses
- Cisco/Webex-locked (Teams support is newer)
- No native Arabic UI
- No AI conversation intelligence (sentiment, summary, topic spotting)
- No omnichannel (voice-only)
- No fraud detection module
- No public pricing transparency for full suite
- Suite can be expensive when bundled

## What to copy
1. **Three recording modes** (Always-On / On-Demand / Selective) — adopt directly
2. **50+ pre-built report library** — accelerates time-to-value
3. **Suite pricing unbundling** — let customers buy modules
4. **Regulated-industry positioning** — same language for SAMA / NCA targeting
5. **Attendant console** as separate module — possible add-on for us
6. **Single subscription with elastic resources** — modern SaaS pricing

## What to beat them on
1. **Multi-PBX (not just Cisco/Webex/Teams)** — we cover 3CX, Zoom, generic SIP too
2. **AI sentiment, summary, topic spotting**
3. **MENA presence + Arabic**
4. **Fraud detection** module
5. **SAMA-compliant** 10-year retention bundle (Italian compliance ≠ Saudi compliance)
6. **MENA payment gateways**
7. **Omnichannel** (P3)

## How to position against them
> "Imagicle is a strong fit for European Cisco/Webex shops in regulated industries (banking, healthcare, law) needing compliance recording. IPT Portal is the MENA equivalent — adding Arabic UI, SAMA compliance, KSA data residency, AI conversation intelligence, fraud detection, and multi-PBX support beyond Cisco's ecosystem."
