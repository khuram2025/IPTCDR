Platform features

Every number you need — and the tools to act on it

Zentryc is a complete contact-center intelligence and telecom-billing suite. Here is everything it does, grouped the way you run your operation.

Call-center analytics

The truth about your queues

Most 3CX reports measure the IVR auto-answer, not the agent — so they flatter you. Zentryc reads the real queue engine.

Genuine ACD KPIs

Answer rate, service level, speed-of-answer (ASA) and average talk time computed from real agent-wait timing.

Abandonment intelligence

True wait-before-abandon, abandoned-call counts, short-abandon thresholds and which agent the call was ringing.

Agent productivity

Per-agent answered vs lost rings, answer rate, AHT and occupancy (talk ÷ logged-in), every metric vs its target.

Per-queue breakdown

Drill into any queue: volume, answered, abandoned, ASA and longest wait over any date range.

Per-call drill-down

Open any call to see its full timeline, parties, routing, cost and voice-quality, plus related calls from the same caller.

Fast at scale

Materialized daily rollups keep date-range dashboards sub-second across millions of call records.

Real-time operations

Run the floor live

Supervisor wallboard

Full-screen, TV-ready board: calls waiting, talking vs connecting, longest current wait and per-queue load, auto-refreshing every few seconds.

SLA breach alerts

Tiered amber/red alerts when answer rate, ASA or abandon-wait crosses your thresholds — per tenant and per queue.

Escalations

Unacknowledged alerts escalate to the right supervisor after a configurable delay — no missed breach.

Email, SMS & WhatsApp

Deliver alerts on the channels your team actually watches, with a full delivery audit trail.

Reporting

Reports that send themselves

Scheduled reports

Daily, weekly or monthly delivery in your timezone — call-center summary, queue performance, agent productivity, SLA breaches and more.

CSV · Excel · PDF · HTML

Every report renders in four formats, branded and ready to forward to stakeholders.

Report catalog

A growing library of prebuilt reports you can run ad-hoc and download instantly, or schedule in two clicks.

Telecom billing

From call cost to paid invoice

Invoicing

Generate per-tenant invoices that reconcile to the summed per-call cost, with line items by call category.

Multi-currency & tax

Bill in the tenant's currency with the correct VAT/tax rule applied automatically by country.

Quota management

Per-extension spend quotas with automatic external-call blocking on the PBX when a balance runs out.

Fraud detection

Toll-fraud rules over call patterns, incident tracking and instant alerts before the bill explodes.

Platform & security

Enterprise-ready, multi-tenant SaaS

Multi-tenant

Strict per-company data isolation — run unlimited tenants from one secure platform.

Security & compliance

Role-based access control, audit logging, IP allow-lists and per-tenant session timeouts.

Modern dashboards

Clean, responsive UI with light & dark modes — usable on the wall, the desk and the phone.

Arabic & RTL

Full right-to-left layout and localization, built for KSA and the GCC.

Multi-vendor core

Vendor-neutral data model — 3CX today, with Cisco CUCM and cloud platforms on the roadmap.

Reliable by design

Background workers, scheduled jobs and idempotent ingestion keep data flowing without touching the hot path.
